Help

Getting started

Use Start free trial on the home page to register. You will get an email with a link to complete setup (including branch selection). You can open the operations app anytime from the home page.

Sign in later

Bookmark your operations app at /app on your domain (e.g. https://yoursite.onrender.com/app#login). Use the username shown after signup (also emailed when mail is configured), or open Open operations app from the home page.

Account & billing

Open Account on this site to view subscription and trial status (requires signing in with your app username and password).

Maintenance tiers, setup/training fees, and cloud vs desktop licensing are in our Pricing Policy (also linked from Terms of Service).
