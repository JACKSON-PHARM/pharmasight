SightOps Pricing Policy

Effective catalogue version: 2026-05-kenya · Currency: Kenya Shillings (KES) · Current list prices

Cloud software, not a one-time desktop licence. SightOps runs in the cloud so you can use multiple devices, keep data backed up, and receive updates without reinstalling. A recurring maintenance (subscription) fee funds servers, security, monitoring, and ongoing development. Optional one-time fees cover human setup and training — they are not a substitute for maintenance.

1. Definitions

  • Organisation — your company account on SightOps.
  • Maintenance / subscription — recurring fee for continued access, hosting, updates, and standard support.
  • Setup (implementation) — one-time services to configure your organisation, branch, users, catalogue, or printers.
  • Training — one-time services to teach your staff; optional and quoted separately unless included in a written offer.
  • Branch — a site operating under a fiscal doctrine (e.g. retail counter, wholesale depot, clinic).
  • Branch credits — internal meter for multi-branch plans (retail = 1 credit, wholesale ≈ 1.5, clinic ≈ 2).

2. Why we charge monthly maintenance

Many Kenyan products sell a one-time fee because they are offline desktop programs: one PC, no shared multi-user cloud, and no ongoing hosting cost for the vendor. SightOps is different — it is cloud-based. Your team can sign in from permitted devices; data is stored and processed on infrastructure we operate; we ship fixes and features without shipping new installers.

The maintenance fee keeps the service available, secure, and improving. If maintenance lapses, access may be suspended after notice, subject to our Terms of Service.

4. Retail Solo vs Retail Pro (feature reservation)

Retail Solo is our most affordable maintenance tier: intended to make SightOps usable for a single small pharmacy with honest limits (users, products, reports). It is designed for owners who can self-setup using tutorials.

Retail Pro is our recommended single-branch tier. It includes the full retail capability we reserve for paying customers: higher limits, extended reports, eTIMS-ready workflows (where enabled for your organisation), and priority support. Features marked as Pro-only on the pricing page are not entitlement on Solo.

We may change tier limits or feature allocation with reasonable notice; published caps at signup apply to your organisation record.

7. Add-ons and upgrades

  • Extra users — beyond tier included users, per published per-user monthly rate.
  • Extra branches — require a higher maintenance tier or branch credit pool; wholesale and clinic branches consume more credits than retail.
  • Custom work — integrations, migrations, or on-site work outside standard packages — quoted in writing.

8. Trials and demos

Trials are time-limited, capped, and not for production reliance. We may convert or require purchase of a paid tier to continue after trial.

9. Taxes and invoices

List prices are VAT-exclusive unless your invoice states otherwise. You are responsible for applicable taxes.

10. Changes to prices

We may update list prices or this policy. Material changes to maintenance for existing paying customers will be communicated with reasonable notice. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance unless your separate contract says otherwise.

11. Refunds

Maintenance is generally non-refundable for elapsed periods. One-time setup/training fees are non-refundable once delivery has started, except where required by law or stated in a written offer.
